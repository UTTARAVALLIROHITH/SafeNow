How to use

Set it up once, in four steps

After setup, SOS is a single action.

  1. Install and allow

    Download the APK, open it, and grant permissions on the “Ready to Protect” screen.

  2. Add your circle

    Save up to 3 contacts and star one as primary.

  3. Choose triggers

    Voice activation, shake to trigger and phrases live in Settings.

  4. Trigger SOS

    Press and hold the SOS button. A 5, 10 or 15 second countdown runs, then your circle is alerted.

Press and hold

Hold the red SOS button on the home screen.

Say your phrase

Turn on Voice Activation. The home screen shows whether Voice Protection is active or paused.

Shake the phone

Turn on Shake to Trigger in Settings. It is a separate switch.

Before you rely on it, tell your trusted contacts about SafeNow and check that Voice Protection shows as active. Review permissions under Settings → Review Permissions, and allow SafeNow to run unrestricted in battery settings.

Features

Built around the first few seconds

SOS, hands-free

Voice SOS activates emergency protocols when you say your phrase, even if the phone is locked. The app ships with 10 emergency phrases, and the home screen shows whether Voice Protection is active or paused.

Shake to Help

In extreme situations, a rapid shake triggers your safety circle. It is a separate switch in Settings, so you decide whether to use it.

Trusted circle

Manage mother, brother, father or anyone you choose. Mark one contact as primary with the star, remove contacts, or add a new one from the plus button. The home screen also shows location, saved contacts and battery at a glance, and a History tab keeps your emergency activity.

Good to know

What SafeNow does, and what it depends on

SafeNow

Starts the countdown, notifies your contacts, shares live GPS and places the emergency calls.

Your phone

Android controls background activity, battery saver and call or SMS access. Denied permissions limit what the app can do.

Network and GPS

Alerts and location need signal. A switched-off phone or empty battery cannot send an SOS.

Always use your local emergency number when you can.

FAQ

Questions

How do I trigger SOS?

Press and hold the SOS button on the home screen, say one of your emergency phrases if Voice Activation is on, or shake the phone if Shake to Trigger is on.

How many contacts can I add?

The Trusted Circle notifies up to 3 contacts. You can mark one as primary.

Can I change the countdown?

Yes. Settings offers 5, 10 or 15 seconds, and lets you edit the SMS template sent to contacts.

Does voice activation work on a locked phone?

The app describes Voice SOS as working even if your phone is locked. Android battery restrictions can affect this, so review permissions after installing.
